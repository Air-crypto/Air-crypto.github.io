# Desk artwork

`workbench.webp` is an original background generated with the built-in image generation tool in generation mode. The generated PNG was converted to WebP at 1536 × 1024 for the site. Interface text and clickable areas are HTML/CSS overlays.

The reference portfolio informed the idea of exploring a desk. No reference artwork or source code was reused.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original interactive portfolio website background, landscape 1536 by 1024. Create a premium editorial photograph of a young computer engineer's personal workbench, directly overhead, perfectly flat lay. Warm honey walnut tabletop fills entire frame. Original arrangement, calm sunny afternoon with soft daylight from upper left, realistic tactile materials and clean shadows. A centered open silver laptop occupies x=33% to 66%, y=22% to 81%; its screen is front-facing rectangular from the top-down camera, solid near-black with a subtle dark teal glow, absolutely NO text or UI, keyboard below it. At upper left x=13%, y=20% an open cream engineering notebook with small abstract hand-drawn circuit traces, a dark green pencil across it. At lower left x=15%, y=68% a tidy stack of two instant photo prints showing a Wisconsin lakeside scene and an ice rink, no people or lettering. At upper right x=82%, y=25% a green printed circuit board with small chips and gold traces, alongside a tiny screwdriver. At lower right x=83%, y=72% a pale yellow blank square sticky note and a dark graphite pen. A small plant barely enters top left corner, a ceramic off-white coffee cup near bottom center-right edge. Keep tabletop uncluttered with warm negative space between objects. A little scuffed and lived-in, understated analog warmth, real wood grain, restrained muted teal and cream palette. No human hands, no logos, no readable text, no branding, no watermarks. Do not imitate any particular portfolio site. Highly realistic, beautiful textures, objects physically plausible and separated so they can be clickable regions.
